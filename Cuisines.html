Punjabi food

Punjabi food is a rich, hearty, and vibrant cuisine from the Punjab region of India and Pakistan, known for its bold flavors, heavy use of dairy like butter, ghee, and paneer, and the iconic use of the clay oven, or tandoor.



Uttarakhand food

Uttarakhand food is simple, earthy, and deeply nourishing, reflecting the rustic charm of the Himalayan hills.



South indian food

South Indian food is a vibrant and healthy culinary tradition defined by the use of rice, lentils, coconut, and aromatic spices.



Gujarati food

known for its unique balance of sweet, spicy, salty, and sour flavors.
